Switch to LIVE mode?

This is not a simulation. The engine will fire real market orders against balances that must already be pre-funded on every connected exchange:

  • Pre-funded balances on both sides of every trade are mandatory — there is no buy-transfer-sell path fast enough to catch a spread.
  • Partial / one-sided fills need manual reconciliation (see execution/reconciler.py).
  • Only trade with capital you can afford to lose.
